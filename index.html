Daily Story Uploader

Daily Story Uploader is the publishing tool behind Daily Moral Stories, a channel that shares a new short animated moral story every day.

What it does

Each day, the tool turns an original short story into an animated vertical video with narration and captions. It then uploads that video to the channel owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Google data it uses

The tool asks for one permission: "Manage your YouTube videos" (scope youtube.upload). It uses that permission only to upload the channel's daily video, with its title, description and tags. It does not read, store or share other YouTube or Google account data.

YouTube API Services

Daily Story Uploader uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3). By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
